The Challenge

Heat is an urban emergency — and a municipal budget crisis.

Cities endure escalating heat waves, spiking peak energy grid stress, and worsening public health disparities. Planners lack a unified intelligence layer to connect satellite thermal data with actionable intervention economics.

🌡️

Rising Urban Heat

Impervious concrete and dark asphalt absorb thermal radiation, creating suffocating microclimates up to 10°C hotter than surrounding rural areas.

⚡

Surging Energy Demands

High temperatures overload city power grids and skyrocket municipal and commercial HVAC cooling operational expenditures.

🏙️

Unequal Heat Vulnerability

Disadvantaged neighborhoods with low tree canopy density bear severe heat burdens, leading to disproportionate health impacts.

💰

Constrained Capital Budgets

Cities cannot afford indiscriminate solutions. Every dollar allocated must target the exact zones with highest cooling return-on-investment.
